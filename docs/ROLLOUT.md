# Rollout and adoption plan (proposed)

1. **Week 1: one seller, real accounts, shadow mode.** The associate runs the desk alongside the current process for one seller's three accounts. Nothing changes for the seller.
2. **Week 2: Monday sync from the desk.** Use the weekly desk as the agenda. Collect what was missing.
3. **Weeks 3–4: all three sellers.** Turn on the capacity rule with the sales leader's agreement. Introduce the route gate as a checklist, not a blocker, for one week; then enforce.
4. **Week 5: events lead joins.** Tie each event seat to a campaign and owner.
5. **Week 6: review.** Read the experiment measures; decide whether the gates move into the CRM as validation rules or stay on the desk.

Adoption signals to watch: sellers opening the brief before an ask; routes moving from hypothesis to verified; stalls fixed within one SLA. Exit criteria: if sellers don't use it by week 3, stop and keep only the five-sentence template.
