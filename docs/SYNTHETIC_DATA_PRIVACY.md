# Synthetic data and privacy plan

- **Eight synthetic accounts** (Halvorsen Mutual, Corriveau Health, Brightwater Financial, Kestrel Aerospace, Meridian Retail Holdings, Ostrander Energy, Vantage Therapeutics, Larchmont Bancorp) are fictional. Any resemblance to real firms is unintended. Every screen and export marks them **Synthetic**.
- **Synthetic people** are seats, not people: "CISO (synthetic seat)". Introducers are coded ("Advisor S-02"). No fictional personal names are invented.
- **Sellers** are "Seller A/B/C". No Harmonic employee is named or implied.
- **One real account**, JPMorganChase, uses only the company's own public pages, read on 2026-10-06. Names shown are those the company publishes for the role. It is a prospect hypothesis; no relationship with Harmonic is claimed or implied.
- **Real Harmonic investors and board** are referenced only as "listed on the About page" and rated *public affiliation only*. No relationship between them and JPMorganChase is claimed.
- **No private data**: no emails, phone numbers, LinkedIn profiles, credentials, private outreach recipients or application documents are stored.
- **Storage**: browser localStorage only; nothing is sent anywhere.
- **Share mode** strips internal route notes, introducer names and any name marked uncertain.
- **Input is data**: text typed into the desk is rendered as text and never executed or used as an instruction; a trust test checks this.
