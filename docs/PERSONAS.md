# Hypothesis personas

These are hypotheses built from the job posting, not interviews. No real people were interviewed.

## Strategic seller ("Seller A")
- Carries a short list of Fortune 100/250 accounts; quota rides on a handful of deals.
- Needs: to know which of their three campaigns is closest to a real conversation, and to trust that an intro ask won't embarrass them.
- Frustration (assumed): being handed "warm" routes that turn out to be a shared logo on an investor page.
- Desk view: their lane on the weekly desk; the brief.

## Strategic GTM Associate (primary user)
- Supports three sellers; owns mapping, signals, briefs and campaign hygiene.
- Needs: one place that says what is due, what is stuck, and what evidence is missing.
- Frustration (assumed): chasing status across threads; being asked for a number with no denominator.
- Desk view: everything; especially Momentum and Trust.

## Marketing / events lead
- Runs executive dinners and roundtables.
- Needs: each seat tied to a named campaign with an owner for the follow-up.
- Desk view: event plan and event-seat tickets on Momentum.

## Sales leader
- Allocates seller capacity and asks for progress.
- Needs: capacity respected (three each), honest measures, no invented pipeline.
- Desk view: weekly desk header, capacity board, measures.
