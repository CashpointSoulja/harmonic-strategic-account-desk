# Five whys (hypothesis)

Starting symptom (assumed, not observed): a strategic campaign goes quiet after a promising executive dinner.

1. **Why did it go quiet?** No one sent the follow-up the week after the dinner.
2. **Why not?** The seller assumed the associate had it; the associate assumed the events lead had it.
3. **Why was ownership unclear?** The event plan lived in one place, the account notes in another, and neither had a single named owner with a date.
4. **Why is there no single owner and date?** Strategic campaigns are few and long, so they are tracked in heads and threads rather than in a system built for nine high-stakes items.
5. **Why does that matter more here than in SDR work?** Each campaign burns a scarce introducer favour or event seat. A stall wastes something that cannot be bought back.

**Design response:** every active campaign must have an owner, a dated next action and an SLA; a missing owner blocks stage movement; the stalled campaign shows its failure reason and the exact fields that fix it. See `Meridian Retail Holdings` in the demo.
