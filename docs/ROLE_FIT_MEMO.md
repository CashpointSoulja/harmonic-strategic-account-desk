# Role-to-feature viability memo

Source: Strategic GTM Associate posting, https://jobs.ashbyhq.com/harmonic-security-inc/8ac03b27-f246-4f4e-8bb0-63b580aa46a3 (read 2026-10-06). Paraphrased; see the research ledger.

| Posting theme (paraphrased) | Feature | Viability | Honest gap |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Support strategic sellers on Fortune 100/250 accounts | Three seller lanes, 9 slots | High: works as a weekly operating view | Seller and account counts are assumptions |
| Warm routes via investors, advisors, partners, customers, team | Route ledger with verification gate | High: the refusal logic is the core value | Real relationship data would come from the team, never from public pages |
| Executive events and roundtables | Event plan + seat tickets | Medium: tracks ownership, not logistics | No guest-list or venue tooling |
| Account mapping | Buying committee + evidence ledger | High | Real committees need internal notes the desk keeps out of share mode |
| Signal tracking | Signal desk with shelf life | Medium: manual entry only | No live feeds (deliberately: no scraping, no paid APIs) |
| Briefing | Five-sentence composer | High | Template wording needs seller tuning |
| Not cold sequences / call targets | No email, no cadence, no call counts | High | — |
| Measure progress | Measures with denominators | Medium | Meetings and pipeline need CRM data; left unmeasured |

**Where the concept would be wrong:** if Harmonic's strategic motion already runs in a CRM with good route fields, the desk's value shrinks to the gates and the brief, which could live as CRM validation rules.
