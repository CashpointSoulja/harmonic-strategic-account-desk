# Experiment and cohort plan (proposed, not run)

No experiment has been run. This is how the desk would be tested if adopted.

**Question.** Does running strategic campaigns through the desk's gates reduce stalls and wasted introducer asks without slowing campaigns down?

**Design.** Two-cohort, within-team, six weeks. With three sellers, randomising sellers is too small for statistics, so randomise *campaigns*: of each seller's new campaigns, alternate assignment to desk-gated vs current process. Expect roughly 6–12 campaigns per arm over six weeks, which is small; treat results as directional.

| Measure | Definition | Window |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Stall rate | campaigns with no touch beyond SLA ÷ campaigns in arm | weekly, 6 weeks |
| Owner gaps | campaign-days with no owner ÷ campaign-days | 6 weeks |
| Declined or unanswered intro asks | asks with no reply in 14 days or declined ÷ asks | 6 weeks + 14 days |
| Time to first executive meeting | days from campaign start to CRM-logged meeting | 6 weeks; right-censored |

**Guardrails.** Seller satisfaction (1–5 weekly pulse, n = 3, read qualitatively); number of asks made (the gate must not simply suppress all asks).

**Stop rule.** Stop the gated arm if asks fall to zero for two consecutive weeks.

**Report.** Every rate with numerator, denominator and window; no significance claims below 30 per arm.
