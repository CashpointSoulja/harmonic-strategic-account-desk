# User stories

1. As the associate, I see every seller's active campaigns and capacity on one screen so I can plan the week.
2. As a seller, I see fit, timing and route readiness separately, with weights, so I understand why a campaign ranks where it does.
3. As the associate, I see Unknown rather than zero when evidence is missing so I don't mistake no data for a bad account.
4. As the associate, I see overdue, no-next-step, no-owner and SLA flags on each campaign.
5. As a seller, I cannot reactivate a parked campaign into a full lane, and I can move it to a lane with room.
6. As the associate, I see each buying committee with decides/influences, coverage and unknown seats.
7. As anyone, I can open the source for every evidence sentence and see its date, confidence and provenance.
8. As a seller, I cannot request an introduction through a public affiliation or hypothesis route.
9. As a seller, I can request (prepare) an introduction only when relationship, permission and recipient are verified and the introducer was not asked in the last 30 days.
10. As the associate, I see stale and unsourced signals rejected as triggers, with the reason.
11. As the associate, duplicate signals are refused when I add them.
12. As a seller, I get a five-sentence brief I can edit; the desk tells me exactly what blocks "ready".
13. As a seller, the brief is blocked if it claims a relationship the ledger hasn't verified.
14. As the associate, I can fix a stalled campaign by assigning an owner and a dated next step, and resume it.
15. As the events lead, I can prepare an event-seat ticket tied to a campaign.
16. As the sales leader, I see measures with numerators, denominators and windows, and CRM outcomes marked Not measured.
17. As anyone, I can export the desk or a brief in share mode with internal notes and uncertain names stripped.
18. As anyone, my changes persist in this browser and Reset demo restores the seeded desk.
